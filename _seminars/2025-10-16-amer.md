abstract: |
  This seminar takes the audience on a journey from the fundamental principles of electronics cooling to breakthrough innovations transforming the field today. Starting with heat transfer physics, it progresses through practical cooling modules and culminates in emerging trends such as fans (blowers), nanofluids, smart materials, and sustainable cooling solutions.
bio: |
  Dr. Mohammed Amer is head of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Palestine Technical University – Kadoorie. He earned his PhD in mechanical science from National Chiao Tung University, Taiwan, and has extensive experience in advanced thermal management, cooling systems, and mechatronic solutions. His research spans artificial intelligence, solar PV/T systems, nanofluids, and aerodynamics, with over 50 publications and 10 patents. Dr. Amer also serves on international editorial boards and professional societies. His achievements have been recognized with multiple awards, including the Best Product Award in Taiwan in 2023 and the ICEISET Best Paper Award in 2023.
