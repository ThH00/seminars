bio: |
  Dr. Chrystal Chern is a UC Berkeley Future of Higher Education Postdoctoral Fellow affiliated with the STructural Artificial Intelligence Research Lab (STAIR Lab), the Dynamics Lab, and the Berkeley Discovery program. She holds a Ph.D. from UC Berkeley, where her doctoral research focused on structural dynamics and digital twin frameworks for civil infrastructure health monitoring — a framework now deployed by the California Department of Transportation. Before her doctorate, she worked as a building enclosure engineer at Simpson Gumpertz & Heger, specializing in acceptance testing and failure diagnosis of building assemblies.

  Her current research bridges mechanical systems, sensing, and computational methods, centering on system identification — extracting models of physical systems from measured data and understanding their reliability for infrastructure health monitoring. She is an active maintainer and developer of the open-source `mdof` system identification package.
