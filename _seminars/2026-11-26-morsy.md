bio: |
  Ahmed Amr Morsy is a postdoctoral researcher in the Nonlinear Dynamics group at ETH Zurich's Institute for Mechanical Systems. His research focuses on creating low-dimensional models that explain and predict nonlinear phenomena such as bifurcations and chaos in high-dimensional models and experiments, with particular emphasis on model reduction, data-driven modeling, invariant manifold reduction, and uncertainty quantification in structural dynamics. He earned his Ph.D. in Mechanical and Process Engineering from ETH Zurich in 2025, with a dissertation on nonlinear model reduction and uncertainty quantification of structural dynamics with a focus on joints, and his M.S. in Civil Engineering from Politecnico di Milano in 2020. Alongside his research, he serves as a teaching assistant for the Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos courses.
