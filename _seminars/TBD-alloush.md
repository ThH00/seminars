  Mhamad Mahdi Alloush's research is directed toward modeling heat transfer and fluid flow phenomena using computational fluid dynamics (CFD). His work concentrates on the development of multiphase flow solvers using pressure-based coupled approaches, non-linear multigrid methods for accelerating solutions in complex engineering problems (particularly turbomachinery applications), and turbulence model implementation. He has been part of AUB's CFD Group, working alongside Professors Fadl Moukalled and Marwan Darwish on the open-source uFVM finite-volume toolbox.
